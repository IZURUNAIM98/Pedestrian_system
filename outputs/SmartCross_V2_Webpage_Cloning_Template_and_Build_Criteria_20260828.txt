======================================================================
1. PURPOSE OF THIS TEMPLATE
======================================================================

Use this document to build another webpage that reproduces the structure,
appearance, behaviour, safety logic, responsive layout, and verification
quality of SmartCross V2 as closely as practical.

This is a functional replication specification, not permission to copy private
credentials, deployment tokens, protected data, trademarks, or third-party
assets. Replace organisation names and deployment identifiers when the cloned
webpage belongs to a different owner.

The clone must remain an educational, deterministic simulation unless a new
project has separately implemented, authorised, tested, and documented real
hardware or external integrations. Do not describe mock CCTV, plate data,
notifications, signals, or incident records as live operational services.

2. AIM METHOD
======================================================================

ACTOR
- Primary: authorised municipal operators, safety planners, reviewers, testers,
  demonstration users, and project stakeholders.
- Secondary: accessibility reviewers, crossing engineers, school safety teams,
  and developers maintaining the simulator.
- System actors: two conflicting vehicles, one pedestrian or a compact student

INPUT

- Crossing mode: Normal or School.
- One selectable crossing condition.
- Deterministic seven-frame motion profile.
- Local timestamps, signal states, route-clear state, sensor state, risk state,

MISSION

- Enforce STOP before WALK.
- Preserve an understandable local event history.
- Demonstrate CCTV plate capture for violations without using a live camera,
  vehicle database, enforcement service, or personal identification.
- Keep critical-event acknowledgement and resolution inside the local mock
  workflow.

4. NON-NEGOTIABLE SAFETY AND EVIDENCE BOUNDARIES
======================================================================

4.1 Core signal invariant
- Pedestrian WALK may appear only when both conflicting vehicle approaches are
  STOP and the protected route is verified clear.
- Reject any state in which pedestrian WALK and conflicting vehicle GO occur at
  the same time.
- When input is stale, obstructed, uncertain, or conflicting, keep pedestrians
  at WAIT/HOLD and send the simulated record to human review.

4.2 Simulation-only requirements
- Use local deterministic mock data.
- No live CCTV feed.
- No automatic number-plate recognition database lookup.
- No physical traffic-light control.
- No enforcement connection.
- No emergency-service dispatch.
- No personal identification.
- No external notification may leave prototype mode.
- Show these boundaries on the access page, main application, evidence panel,
  critical workflow, PDF report, and footer.

4.3 Evidence requirements
- Plate numbers must be synthetic and deterministically generated.
- Confidence must be labelled mock or simulated.
- Violation outcomes require authorised human review.
- Possible distraction must remain suspected or unverified, never confirmed by
  the simulator alone.
- Browser-memory records must not be presented as evidence of a real offence.

12. CONDITION CATALOGUE
======================================================================

Counting rule
- Normal: 20 selectable conditions = 1 no-violation baseline + 19 violations.
- School: 21 selectable conditions = 1 no-violation baseline + 20 violations.
- Condition 17, exceeded school-zone speed limit, is School-only.
- Across both modes there are 39 selectable violation profiles: 19 Normal and
  20 School, plus two mode-specific no-violation baselines.

Safe baseline (first option in each mode)
- Normal pedestrian crossing: no violation.
- School pedestrian crossing: no violation.
- Both opposing vehicles approach, decelerate, stop before their lines, and
  remain STOP while the pedestrian or students receive WALK.
- WALK lasts through a verified protected interval; both modes use a 12-second
  protected WALK in the reference build.
- Traffic resumes only after the route clears.
- No CCTV plate is captured for the no-violation condition.

Violation catalogue and required visual effect
01. Failure to stop or give way
    Effect: conflict path and STOP/HOLD response.
02. Crossing-zone speeding
    Effect: speed-threshold breach; School uses lower context-sensitive threshold.
03. Red-signal violation
    Effect: red signal and stop-line breach.
04. Stopped or parked on the crossing
    Effect: marked crossing obstruction; actor remains fixed until reset.
05. Accessible route blocked
    Effect: accessible-route obstruction and wheelchair/accessible user context.
06. Motorcycle filtering at the crossing
    Effect: filtering path through a vehicle queue.
07. Motorcycle in pedestrian area
    Normal: motorcycle intrusion into pedestrian area.
    School: refined as premature pupil movement contained before the zebra.
08. Overtaking a yielding vehicle
    Effect: yielding-vehicle overtake path.
09. Driving in the wrong direction
    Effect: wrong-direction path.
10. Opposing-lane queue bypass
    Effect: opposing-lane bypass and queue.
11. Unsafe lane change near the crossing
    Effect: danger-zone lane change.
12. Possible distracted driving or phone use
    Effect: observable delayed/distraction path; always unverified.
13. Tailgating near the stop line
    Effect: unsafe following-gap marker.
14. Aggressive acceleration or braking
    Effect: aggressive speed change; School refinement emphasises emergency braking.
15. Unsafe U-turn near the crossing
    Effect: smooth U-turn arc; use longer frame timing where needed.
16. Failure to yield to a vulnerable pedestrian
    Effect: vulnerable-user priority route and longer clearance where required.
17. Exceeded school-zone speed limit
    School only. Effect: 30 km/h school-zone boundary and detected-speed text.
18. Parking obstructing sight distance
    Effect: blocked sight-distance triangle; School ties it to gate/keep-clear area.
19. Collision with a road user or infrastructure
    Effect: collision trajectory, contact, impact point, critical alarm.
20. Vehicle fire, smoke, or explosion
    Effect: fire indicator, separate FIRE / SMOKE and STOP signs, exclusion zone,
    frozen critical state, and mock emergency workflow.

Each Normal and School violation must have its own assigned data profile. A
clone does not pass acceptance if all options merely change labels while using
one generic vehicle animation.

13. SEVEN-STAGE MOTION AND CONTROLLER MODEL
======================================================================

Stage names
1. Approach
2. Initiation
3. Detected
4. Escalation
5. Conflict
6. Response
7. Outcome

Every stage must record
- Timeline stage number and name.
- Vehicle A and B: x position, lane position, heading, speed, state, label, and
  optional visibility.
- Pedestrian: progress, state, and type.
- Vehicle signal: GO, AMBER, or STOP.
- Pedestrian signal: WAIT or WALK.
- Controller state.
- Path tone: blue, amber, red, or green.
- Scenario effect.
- Route-clear Boolean.
- Active sensor.
- Risk: Normal, Caution, High, or Emergency.
- Plain-language action.
- Duration and optional protected-WALK seconds at frame start.

Allowed vehicle states
- WAITING, APPROACHING, ACCELERATING, BRAKING, STOPPED, YIELDING, TURNING,
  WRONG_WAY, BLOCKING, COLLISION, FIRE, CLEARED.

Allowed pedestrian states
- WAITING, WALKING, PAUSED, VULNERABLE_PRIORITY, ROUTED_AWAY, COMPLETED, HOLD.

Controller states
- DETECTION, VERIFICATION, CAUTION, WALK, HOLD, STOP_ALL, CRITICAL_ALARM,
  OUTCOME, RESET.

Timing
- Standard frame duration target: 1000 ms.
- U-turn frame duration target: 1800 ms where the smooth arc needs more time.
- Controller display updates approximately every 250 ms but counts real seconds.
- Both roadside pedestrian timers must show the same shared countdown.
- Do not create independent timers that can drift.

Motion requirements
- Actors remain stationary before Run.
- Movement between frames is continuous and physically understandable.
- Heading changes must not rotate text labels or safety signs upside down.
- Stopped actors finish at zero speed.
- Blocking, collision, and fire scenarios remain frozen until reset where the
  scenario requires manual clearance.
- Any violation recovery that permits WALK must first place both vehicles at a
  red stop line and verify routeClear=true.
- Critical collision and fire outcomes remain pedestrian WAIT/HOLD.

15. CCTV AND SYNTHETIC PLATE EVIDENCE
======================================================================

Applies to both Normal and School violation scenarios.

Required sequence
- Camera begins in Camera ready state.
- At stage 3, Detected, camera changes to active capture state.
- Generate one deterministic synthetic plate from scenario ID and mode.
- Recommended format: SCV2-N#### for Normal and SCV2-S#### for School.
- Display the plate in the road scene and the evidence card.
- Preserve camera ID, capture timestamp, synthetic plate, mock confidence, and
  SIMULATED_CAPTURE status in the result.

Evidence card fields
- Camera: CCTV V2-01 or clone-specific mock identifier.
- Synthetic plate.
- Mock confidence percentage.
- Status: Local review only.

No-violation behaviour
- Camera may remain visibly ready.
- Do not generate or display a plate capture.

Mandatory boundary copy
- State that CCTV and plate data are simulated.
- State that no live camera, database lookup, enforcement, or personal
  identification is used.

======================================================================
16. CRITICAL INCIDENT AND MOCK NOTIFICATION WORKFLOW
======================================================================

Critical conditions
- Collision.
- Vehicle fire, smoke, or explosion.

Critical response
- Both vehicles STOP.
- Pedestrian HOLD/WAIT.
- Display Critical incident banner.
- Play three short local alarm tones once per session if audio is supported.
- Do not replay the alarm repeatedly during React re-rendering.
- Require Reset simulation before replay.
- Queue only fictional local notifications.

Fictional recipient list
- Municipal control centre: fictional test recipient.
- Police: mock queue only.
- Fire and rescue: mock queue only.
- Emergency medical/public hospital liaison: mock queue only.

Workflow steps
1. Detected.
2. Verification required.
3. Preliminary alert issued.
4. Mock notification recorded.
5. Acknowledged.
6. Incident resolved.

User actions
- Acknowledge mock alert.
- Resolve mock workflow.
- Confirm for local record.
- Mark false positive.

These actions update only local state. They must never send a message or contact
an external recipient.

17. HISTORY, COUNTERS, LOGS, AND REPORTS
======================================================================

Session history
- Keep the newest session first.
- Reference maximum: 12 browser-session results.
- Preserve history when a sequence is reset.
- History resets naturally when browser state is lost unless persistence is
  separately designed and disclosed.

Eight simulator counters
1. Total violations.
2. Failure to yield.
3. Speed violations.
4. Crossing obstructions.
5. Motorcycle violations.
6. Critical incidents.
7. Normal crossing.
8. School crossing.

Event log
- One stage-aligned entry per frame: seven entries per completed simulation.
- Categories: sensor, detection, controller, safety, outcome.
- Include timestamp, stage, actor states/speeds, pedestrian state, sensor, risk,
  controller, signals, synthetic plate capture at Detected, and final boundary.
- Reveal entries progressively while the animation is running.
- Filters: All events, Sensor, Detection, Controller, Safety, Outcome.
- Clear log hides violation entries from the local view without altering other
  current-session counters or records.

Reports view
- Table columns: Session, Scenario, Mode, Confidence, Outcome, Completed (MYT).
- Download a readable, paginated PDF.
- Suggested filename: smartcross-v2-simulation-report.pdf.
- PDF includes generation time, summary counts, session history, detection,
  simulated CCTV evidence, outcome, and evidence boundary.
- PDF must not imply real evidence or operational integration.

22. BUILD SEQUENCE FOR A CLONE
======================================================================

Phase 1 - Remember and understand
1. Confirm the new owner, product name, representative location, and whether the
   project remains simulation-only.
2. Inventory the required Normal and School conditions.
3. Confirm which brand terms may be reused and which must be replaced.
4. Freeze the safety invariant and evidence boundary before styling.

Phase 2 - Apply
5. Create the App Router structure and shared UI primitives.
6. Implement the access gate and protected API routes.
7. Implement typed scenario, motion, timeline, signal, CCTV, and event models.
8. Build the application shell and seven page views.
9. Build the Normal crossing scene first, including safe baseline.
10. Add the School layout only where context requires it: taller frontage,
    school/courtyard, student group, lower speed context, and condition 17.

Phase 3 - Analyse
11. Implement each violation as a distinct seven-frame motion sequence.
12. Synchronise signals, timers, timeline, overlays, logs, and CCTV capture to the
    same frame/controller clock.
13. Check all STOP/WAIT/WALK and route-clear combinations.
